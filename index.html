Where do I get it?
  1. On airbnb.com, open Menu > Earnings in the owner's account (or the listing's earnings, if you're a co-host with full access). Your own co-host earnings page only has your payouts, not the stays.
  2. Choose Paid (or Transaction history). Set the dates to cover the month and the month before, so stays that started then are complete.
  3. Pick the listings, then Export CSV. Drop that file above. One file can hold several owners: you tick each owner's listings in step 2.

How the numbers work

What is "Gross earnings" and what is "after Airbnb's fee"?

In Airbnb's export, Gross earnings is what the stay earned for the host before Airbnb's host service fee: the nightly rate, the cleaning fee and any other fees you charge. After the fee is Gross earnings minus the service fee, which is what Airbnb actually pays out. Co-host agreements use both, so pick the one in yours. If your % is not charged on cleaning, leave "Take the % on the cleaning fee too" off.

How does a stay across two months get split?

With "Split by night", a stay from the 29th to the 2nd with 4 nights puts 3 nights in the first month and 1 in the next. The nightly money and Airbnb's fee are split in that proportion; the cleaning fee is one job, so it stays whole in the check-in month. The two months always add up to the stay, to the cent. Refunds and adjustments land in the month Airbnb dated them, as their own line. Long stays that Airbnb pays monthly are counted by payment date.

I'm on Airbnb's split payout. Why is there still a balance?

Airbnb sends your share straight to you, so the statement takes off what Airbnb already paid you. What is left is what your agreement adds on top: expenses you paid, a monthly fee, or a share Airbnb's split doesn't cover. On a split payout the statement follows Airbnb's payout dates, so a stay across two months doesn't make the balance swing back and forth. The box under the statement compares what Airbnb paid you with your terms, so you can catch a wrong split setting.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. The CSV is read inside this page and saved only in this browser. Unlocking Pro checks the email you paid with; nothing else is sent. The page counts visits anonymously.
